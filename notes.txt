microphone: captures sound waves
sound card: hardware/controller which handles sound waves and writes into audio devices
audio devices: interfaces provided by sound card (basically addresses where sound card writes 
            into, it can be multiple depending on audio input, also it can be used for output 
            too where speakers read from there)
ALSA: linux's low level audio interface which communicates directly with sound cards
Pipewire/ Pulse Audio: Higher level audio interface(communicates with ALSA)
PortAudio: Higher level audio interface than Pipewire(deals OS specific things)
Sounddevice: Surface level interface which let's us access sound devices(communicates with PortAudio)
Channel: The flow of audio. The flow can be one line or two line(mono or stereo). All this processings are done on channels

The flow is
microphone->soundcard-> audiodevices-> ALSA-> Pipewire-> PortAudio -> Sounddevice -> Application
